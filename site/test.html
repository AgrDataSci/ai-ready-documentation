Test maniva

maniva is in alpha. Ask it real questions from your work, see where it helps and where it fails, and tell us. It takes about 20 minutes, and you need no account other than your AI assistant.

Before you start

  • Do not upload unpublished or sensitive data. Your assistant's own privacy terms apply to anything you share with it.
  • Treat answers as a starting point. Check anything you plan to act on against the cited source.
  • Questions about access or setup go to k.desousa@cgiar.org.

  1. Open an AI assistant that can read the web

    ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot all work when web access is on. Start a new conversation so earlier chats do not mix in.

  2. Share the maniva link

    Paste the link, then ask your first question in the same message.

  3. Ask questions from your own work

    The best tests are questions you actually have. If you need a start, try one from each group.

    Evidence
    How many farms does a tricot trial need?
    What do farmers' rankings add to yield data?
    Protocols
    Which survey questions and traits should my bean trial use?
    What are the steps to run a tricot trial from start to finish?
    Data
    Which trials and datasets exist for sorghum?
    Load the common bean trials and summarise them by country.
    Analysis
    How should I analyse trials of my crop?
    How do I link farmer rankings with rainfall data?

  4. Check what you get

    A good maniva answer does three things. Watch for answers that do not.

    • It cites its source. Findings name the paper, dataset or protocol they come from, with a link you can open.
    • It says what it does not know. When maniva has no answer, the assistant should say so instead of inventing one.
    • It keeps the layers apart. Evidence, protocols, data and methods are named as such, so you can tell a published finding from a suggestion.

  5. Tell us how it went

    The feedback form takes about 5 minutes. It includes a prompt that asks your assistant to write a short report of the session with personal details removed. You check the report before sharing it.
